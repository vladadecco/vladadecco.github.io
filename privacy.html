Privacy Policy

How Pufi & Bobo Publisher, our internal publishing tool, handles data.

HomePrivacy PolicyTerms of Service

Last updated: 16 October 2026

This policy covers Pufi & Bobo Publisher and this website. Pufi & Bobo Publisher uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the channel owner is also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What we collect

  • From viewers and children: nothing. The tool never reads comments, viewer accounts, viewing history or any personal data. This website has no cookies, no analytics and no forms.
  • From the channel owner: one OAuth refresh token for our own YouTube channel, so the tool can upload our episodes.
  • From YouTube: the IDs of the videos and playlists we create, and, once a week, public titles and view counts of popular kids videos, used only to choose episode topics.

How we store it

  • The OAuth token is kept as an encrypted secret in our private GitHub repository and is used only by the scheduled publishing job.
  • The IDs of our own videos and playlists are kept in the same private repository, so episodes are not repeated.
  • Public data about other videos is kept for at most 30 days and then replaced by the next weekly refresh.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer any data to anyone.

Revoking access and deleting data

The channel owner can revoke the tool's access at any time at Google security settings. When access is revoked, or on request to the contact address below, we delete the stored token and all stored API data within 7 days.

Children

Our videos are made for children, so they are uploaded as “made for kids”. We do not collect any information from or about children.
