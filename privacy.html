Privacy Policy – Desene Pufi Bobo

Last updated: October 15, 2026

What the app is

"Desene Pufi Bobo" is a private, personal tool run by its owner (GitHub user vladadecco). It automatically creates original animated cartoons for children and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel(s). It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Data accessed through Google / YouTube

  • The app uses the YouTube Data API only with the owner's own Google account, to upload videos, set thumbnails, upload captions and add videos to playlists on the owner's channel.
  • It reads public statistics (titles and view counts) of public YouTube videos to understand which topics are popular. It does not download or reuse other people's videos.
  • No personal data of other people is collected, stored, sold or shared.

Storage and security

Authorization tokens are stored only as encrypted secrets in a private GitHub repository controlled by the owner and are used solely to perform the uploads described above.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account page: myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
