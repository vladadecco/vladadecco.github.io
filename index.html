Pufi & Bobo

Original educational cartoons for children aged 2–6, in Romanian and English. Pufi (a fluffy, curious friend) and Bobo (a cheeky little orange robot) help little ones learn colors, numbers and shapes through stories, songs and games.

"Desene Pufi Bobo" is the owner's personal tool that creates these cartoons and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel. Characters, stories and music are all original.
